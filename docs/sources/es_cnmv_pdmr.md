# `es_cnmv_pdmr`: Spain, CNMV, notifications of managers and closely associated persons

| | |
|---|---|
| Dataset | `insider_transactions` |
| Authority | Comisión Nacional del Mercado de Valores (CNMV), Spain |
| Register | [Notificación de directivos y personas vinculadas (Reglamento de Ejecución (UE) 2016/523)](https://www.cnmv.es/portal/Consultas/Directivos-Consulta.aspx) |
| Listing | `GET https://www.cnmv.es/portal/consultas/directivos-resultado.aspx?fechad=DD/MM/YYYY&fechah=DD/MM/YYYY&page=N` (the search form redirects to this URL; `page` is 0-based, 10 rows per page) |
| Notification | `GET https://www.cnmv.es/webservices/verdocumento/ver?e=<token>` (the token is taken from the listing) |
| Format | Listing: HTML. Notification: PDF generated by the CNMV from its electronic form, with the 2016/523 template and bilingual Spanish/English labels |
| Coverage | Notifications registered since 11 May 2018 (the CNMV electronic procedure "NOD"). Earlier notifications live in a separate consultation ("anteriores al Reglamento de Ejecución (UE) 2016/523") that this connector does not read |
| Terms | No reuse licence is stated in the [legal notice](https://www.cnmv.es/portal/utilidades/NotaLegal.aspx); the CNMV publishes these notifications under MAR Article 19(3). Attribute "CNMV". The site's robots.txt only disallows `*.shtml` |

## Fields
Listing rows: registration date (`dd/mm/yyyy`, no time), issuer and its NIF, declarant, reason for the
notification (PDMR or closely associated person), registration number, document link.

The PDF follows Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/523: 1a name of the declarant, 2 reason (two check boxes),
2a job title, 2b initial notification or amendment ("Inicial" / "Modificación"), 3a issuer, 3b LEI, then one
text line per transaction: `<ISIN> <instrument> <nature> <dd/mm/yyyy> <venue MIC> <volume> <price> <currency>`,
e.g. `ES0000000000 Acción Compra 08/10/2026 XMAD 371,00 55,00 EUR`. Section 5 repeats the aggregated volume
and price per table, and "Otra información" holds free text.

## Mapping and privacy
- One record per transaction line; `record_id` is the registration number plus the line index.
- `published_at` is the registration date at midnight in Madrid (the listing gives no time), stored in UTC.
- Venues are MIC codes; `XOFF` and `XXXX` mean outside a trading venue (`on_venue = false`).
- The instrument and the nature share one text line; known CNMV instrument names ("Acción", "Derechos de
  suscripción", "Opción", ...) are split off first.
- Natural persons are pseudonymized. Legal entities that notify as closely associated persons keep their name.
- For closely associated persons the job title field usually names the manager they are linked to, in free
  form ("CONSEJERO - <name>", "<name>, PRESIDENTE"). The connector pseudonymizes the first name-like part as
  the PDMR and stores only the normalized role, never this text (`pdmr_role_raw` is null). For managers' own
  notifications the job title is stored with any word of the declarant's name redacted.
- "Otra información" (free text that names people) and the amendment details are never stored.

## Quirks
- Nature "Otros" (other) is common for gifts, transfers between spouses and in-kind contributions; the
  explanation is only in the free text, so these rows keep `nature = other`, `direction = 0`.
- Some declarants type the issuer with its tax id ("Example Group, SA; NIF: A00000000"); the suffix is removed.
- Some identification codes are not valid ISINs (missing a character); `isin` is then null.
- A window with a single page of results has no pagination label.
- A form the parser does not recognize is still stored in bronze and reported as an ingest warning.

## Observed (September 2026)
111 notifications, 233 transactions for 37 issuers; 121 transactions by closely associated persons, 1 amendment,
0 missing LEIs, 12 transactions without a valid ISIN; no parser failure.
