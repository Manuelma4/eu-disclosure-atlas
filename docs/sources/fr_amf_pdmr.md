# `fr_amf_pdmr`: France, Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF), managers' transactions

| | |
|---|---|
| Dataset | `insider_transactions` |
| Authority | Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF), France |
| Register | BDIF, "Base des décisions et informations financières": https://bdif.amf-france.org/fr, type of information "Déclarations des dirigeants" (`DD`) |
| Listing | `GET https://bdif.amf-france.org/back/api/v1/informations` (JSON) |
| Parameters | `TypesInformation=DD`, `DateDebut` and `DateFin` (ISO 8601 instants in UTC, inclusive, filter on the publication date), `From` (offset), `Size` (page size; the connector uses 100) |

| Format | One PDF per notification, generated by the AMF from the declarant's ONDE form: the EU template, in French and then in English |
| Coverage | `DD` publications from February 2021 onwards (none returned before that date in our probes); about 4,600 to 4,900 a year (4,564 in 2021, 4,767 in 2023, 4,891 in 2025) |

## Legal basis
France uses the option in Article 19(3) of the Market Abuse Regulation (EU) No 596/2014: notifications are sent
to the AMF, which publishes them (Article L. 621-18-2 of the Code monétaire et financier). Since the SEC's
conditional exemption of 5 March 2026 for insiders of EEA foreign private issuers (Holding Foreign Insiders
Accountable Act), the AMF publishes every French notification in French and in English
([AMF, 28 August 2026](https://www.amf-france.org/fr/actualites-publications/actualites/lamf-publie-desormais-les-declarations-des-dirigeants-en-francais-et-en-anglais)).

## How the connector works
1. **Listing.** For each 7-day window (Paris calendar days, sent as UTC instants), page through
   `informations` 100 items at a time until `total` is reached. Each page is stored in bronze as JSON.
2. **Documents.** Each item has one PDF (`documents[]`); the connector downloads it and stores it in bronze with
   the listing metadata (`numero`, `datePublication`, issuer names) in the manifest.
3. **Parsing.** `pdfplumber` extracts the text; a rule-based reader of the template (`parse_template`) reads
   the first language version (French) into an intermediate `ExtractedFiling`; the connector maps it to one
   `InsiderTransaction` per price and volume line.
4. **Fallback.** If the rules cannot read a document and an LLM is configured, the model's answer is stored in
   bronze next to the PDF (`kind: llm_extraction`, parser `llm:<model>@<prompt-version>`, per-field
   confidence; see [`disclosure_atlas.extraction`](../../src/disclosure_atlas/extraction/)). Without an LLM the
   document is skipped and reported as a warning by `ingest`. On the 1,003 filings published between
   22 July and 20 October 2026, the rules read 1,003 (100%), so the fallback is a safety net, not a
   dependency.

## Quirks
- **One person field.** The template prints the declarant as `NAME, position`. A closely associated person
  appears as `ENTITY personne morale liée à MANAGER, position` (English version: `legal person closely
  associated with`) or `PERSON personne liée à MANAGER, …`. The connector splits it: the notifier is the
  entity or person, the PDMR is the manager (`pdmr_id` = the manager's pseudonym, the same as on the
  manager's own filings for that issuer), `closely_associated` is true. Positions describe the manager.
- **Legal entities.** "personne morale" marks a legal entity; otherwise the name heuristic of `privacy.py`
  decides. Legal forms are often printed twice (`… SE SE`), and the duplicate is removed. An entity named
  after the manager is kept pseudonymous.
- **Free text.** Positions, natures and instrument descriptions are typed by the declarant. Before storage,
  the connector drops whatever follows a relationship word (`M.`, `Mme`, `épouse de`, `contrôlée par`, …)
  in a position and replaces any word of a natural person's name with `[redacted]`, unless the word is part of
  the issuer's or the entity's name (families often give their name to the company). Amendment explanations
  and comments are not stored at all.
- **Amendments** are rare (1 in 1,003 filings over 90 days) and can carry a free-text explanation instead of
  the word "Modification". Anything other than "Notification initiale" counts as an amendment. The AMF
  publishes an amendment as a new filing number, with no link to the filing it corrects, so nothing is marked
  superseded.
- **Several price lines** per transaction (fills at different prices) are common (184 of 1,003 filings). One
  record per line keeps `amount` exact; the aggregated price and volume are checked but not stored. One
  filing had an aggregated volume of 0 against 74,805 in its lines (a declarant error).
- **Currencies** are printed in words (`Euro`, `Livre Sterling`, `Dollar des États-Unis (Même jour)`) and
  mapped to ISO 4217. The aggregated currency is sometimes wrong (USD lines, EUR aggregate); the lines win.
- **ISINs** are typed by the declarant: missing (337 of 1,192 transactions over 90 days), invalid (39: tickers
  such as `PWG`, wrong length) or noise around a valid code (`FR0012613610 – ALPWG`, `xparFR0010557264`). The connector takes the first
  valid ISIN in the field; failing that, for shares only (not depositary receipts), the reference ISIN of the
  filing's header. That leaves 39 of 2,109 records without an ISIN.
- **LEIs** are typed too: 200 of 1,003 filings (20%) have a missing or invalid issuer LEI (bad length,
  checksum, an ISIN in the LEI field). They are left empty; WS5 maps issuers to LEIs.
- **Venues** are free text: `Euronext Paris`, `Multiplaces`, lists of MIC codes, `Dark Pools`,
  `MTF hors Dark Pools` (a venue), `Hors plateforme de négociation` (off venue), `over the counter`.
- **Dates** are printed in French ("16 octobre 2026") and English; some transaction dates are typos (a 2027
  date in a 2026 filing) and are kept as printed.
- **Units of employee savings funds** (FCPE, FIE) and life-insurance units appear as instruments; they are
  `instrument_type = other`, so they never count as open-market share trades.
- **Paging.** The API is backed by a search index: `total` is capped at 10,000 and an offset past 10,000
  returns HTTP 500, so the connector pages within 7-day windows (about 100 filings each). Results are sorted
  by publication date, newest first; a filing published while a window is being paged can make the next
  page repeat one item, which deterministic record ids absorb.
- **Unavailable documents.** The website reports HTTP 451 for documents the AMF withdrew; the connector
  logs and skips 404, 410 and 451.
- **No robots.txt.** `https://bdif.amf-france.org/robots.txt` returns the single-page application, not a
  robots file; `www.amf-france.org/robots.txt` only restricts the CMS directories. The connector identifies
  itself and keeps to one request per second.

## Terms of use
The BDIF legal notice ([mentions légales](https://bdif.amf-france.org/fr/mentions-legales), "Droit d'auteur &
copyright") states that information may be used only for personal, educational, associative or professional
purposes, that commercial or advertising use is excluded, and that electronic reproduction is allowed if the
source, the date of last update and the mention "©Autorité des marchés financiers – droits réservés" are
given and the data is not altered in a misleading way. Hence:
- Raw PDFs stay in the private bronze layer.
- Published records cite the AMF and link each filing (`source_url`). Releases must carry the AMF mention and
  the snapshot date.
- This project is non-commercial research; any commercial reuse of AMF-derived data needs the AMF's
  permission (contact the webmaster, as the notice says).

## Privacy
Notifications name natural persons (managers and their relatives). Names are pseudonymized per issuer
(`ctx.person_id`) and never stored in silver or gold; see [ADR 0002](../decisions/0002-pseudonymize-natural-persons.md).
The LLM fallback sends the document text, names included, to the configured provider: prefer a local model
(Ollama) or a provider under a data-processing agreement, and leave it off (the default) otherwise.

## Observed (90 days, 22 July to 20 October 2026)
1,003 filings from 148 issuers, 2,109 records, all read by the rule-based parser; 0 records without a
transaction date, 39 without an ISIN, 365 (17%) without a valid issuer LEI; 1,122 records from closely
associated persons; median filing lag 4 days, 32% of records published more than five calendar days after the
transaction. Details: the "Live run" section of [evals/fr_amf_pdmr/README.md](../../evals/fr_amf_pdmr/README.md).
